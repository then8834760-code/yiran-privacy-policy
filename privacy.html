概述

以然收藏是一款浏览器新标签页与书签管理扩展。本政策说明扩展在设备本地保存哪些数据、何时访问外部服务以及您可以如何控制相关功能。

本地数据

扩展使用浏览器书签接口读取、创建、重命名、移动和删除书签；这些操作会修改浏览器原有书签。个性化设置、置顶内容、搜索引擎设置、备忘录、纪念日、图标缓存等主要保存于 chrome.storage.local、localStorage 或 IndexedDB。您选择的本地壁纸也可能保存在 IndexedDB。扩展代码未使用 chrome.storage.sync 来同步这些设置。浏览器自身的书签同步功能不受本扩展控制。

权限及用途

必需权限：storage（保存设置及本地数据）、bookmarks（显示及管理书签）。可选权限：tabs（在授权后读取打开的标签页信息以缓存网站图标）、对网站的访问权限 <all_urls>（在授权后请求网页或图标以改善图标显示）。扩展将新标签页替换为自己的首页。已声明的特定网站访问权限用于天气、地理位置估算、节假日、汇率、热榜、壁纸、AI 模型列表和图标获取等功能。

外部网络请求

部分功能会直接连接第三方服务，包括 Open-Meteo（天气、地理编码）、ipwho.is / GeoJS / ipapi.co（根据 IP 估算地区）、Nager Holidays（节假日）、open.er-api.com（汇率）、api.vvhan.com（热榜）、Bing（壁纸）、OpenRouter（公开模型列表）、favicon.im / icon.horse（网站图标）。图标功能也可能向目标网站请求页面或图标。请求可能向相应服务披露您的 IP 地址、请求时间、所请求的域名、位置坐标或地区信息（取决于功能）；第三方会依据其各自政策处理数据。扩展提供搜索入口；提交搜索词时，搜索词将发送给您选择的搜索引擎。点击外部 AI 工具链接时，您将离开扩展并受目标网站政策约束。

位置与天气

天气功能可能使用浏览器定位接口获取坐标（由浏览器请求定位授权），也可能通过 IP 地理位置服务估算位置；天气查询会向天气服务传送所需坐标。您可以通过浏览器的定位权限及扩展设置控制相关使用。

数据共享、保留与删除

在已检查的扩展代码中，未发现将完整书签库或本地备忘录统一上传到开发者服务器的实现，也未发现专门的广告追踪或分析 SDK。这不等于所有网络请求都不传送信息。图标缓存等本地数据可能持续保留，直到您手动清理、重置浏览器数据或卸载扩展；卸载时的清除行为取决于浏览器。删除或修改浏览器书签可能影响原始书签及浏览器同步的副本。

您的选择

您可以在浏览器扩展管理页面撤销可选权限、管理定位权限或卸载扩展；可在扩展中调整功能设置，并在浏览器中管理书签。使用第三方服务相关功能前，请考虑其隐私政策。

儿童、更新与联系

本扩展并非专为儿童设计。本政策可能随着功能、权限或服务提供商变化而更新。联系渠道请使用扩展发布页面所列的开发者联系方式。

Privacy Policy

Yiran Bookmark Hub · 3.17.170 · 2026-11-22

Clear information about how your data is used.

Overview

Yiran Bookmark Hub is a browser new-tab and bookmark management extension. This policy explains which information is stored on your device, when external services are contacted, and how you can control those features.

Locally stored data

The extension uses the browser bookmarks API to read, create, rename, move and delete bookmarks; these actions change your browser bookmarks. Preferences, pinned items, search engine settings, memos, anniversaries, icon caches and similar information are primarily stored using chrome.storage.local, localStorage or IndexedDB. Wallpapers selected from your device may also be stored in IndexedDB. The inspected extension code does not use chrome.storage.sync for these settings. Any bookmark synchronization provided by your browser is outside the extension’s control.

Permissions and purposes

Required permissions: storage (save settings and local data) and bookmarks (display and manage bookmarks). Optional permissions: tabs (after permission is granted, inspect open tabs to cache site icons) and access to websites via <all_urls> (after permission is granted, request pages or icons to improve icon display). The extension replaces the browser’s new-tab page. Declared access to specific service domains supports weather, approximate location, holidays, exchange rates, trending lists, wallpapers, AI model listings and site icons.

External network requests

Some features connect directly to third parties, including Open-Meteo (weather/geocoding), ipwho.is / GeoJS / ipapi.co (IP-based approximate location), Nager Holidays (holidays), open.er-api.com (exchange rates), api.vvhan.com (trending lists), Bing (wallpapers), OpenRouter (public model listings), and favicon.im / icon.horse (website icons). Icon retrieval may also request pages or icons from target websites. Depending on the feature, requests may disclose your IP address, request time, requested domain, coordinates or approximate region to the receiving service. Those providers handle information under their own policies. Search terms are sent to your selected search engine when you submit a search. Opening links to external AI tools takes you outside the extension and subjects your activity there to those sites’ policies.

Location and weather

The weather feature may request coordinates through the browser geolocation API (subject to browser permission), or estimate a location using IP geolocation services. Weather queries send the required coordinates to the weather provider. You can manage related access through browser location permissions and extension settings.

Sharing, retention and deletion

In the inspected extension code, we did not find functionality that uploads the full bookmark library or local memos to a developer-operated server, nor a dedicated advertising tracking or analytics SDK. This does not mean network requests disclose no information. Local data such as icon caches may remain until manually cleared, browser data is reset, or the extension is uninstalled; uninstall cleanup depends on the browser. Deleting or editing bookmarks can affect original bookmarks and any browser-synced copies.

Your choices

You can revoke optional permissions, manage location access, or uninstall the extension through your browser. You can change extension settings and manage bookmarks in the browser. Review the relevant third-party privacy policies before using features that rely on them.

Children, changes and contact

The extension is not specifically designed for children. This policy may be updated when features, permissions or service providers change. For inquiries, use the developer contact information on the extension’s official listing.
